# SACHIKA CLOTHING 3D — Royal Ethnic Couture E-Commerce Platform

A high-converting, interactive 3D online clothing boutique specializing in **Short Kurtis**, **Long Kurtis / Anarkalis**, and **3-Piece Suit Ensembles**, with multi-platform integration across **Flipkart**, **Meesho**, and **Ajio**.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights

### 1. Interactive 3D Mannequin & Fabric Physics Studio
- Real-time 3D WebGL showroom powered by **Three.js**
- True-to-scale mannequin dress forms tailored for:
  - **Short Kurti** (hip length with side slits & gold gota lace)
  - **Long Kurti / Anarkali** (voluminous 32-kali flare with Banarasi zari borders)
  - **3pc Suit Ensemble** (tailored kurti + straight silk pants + flowing translucent organza dupatta drape)
- 360° Drag & Orbit, Zoom, Lighting switchers (Royal Boutique Gold, Daylight, Candlelight), and live silk colorway preview.

### 2. Multi-Store Availability (Flipkart, Meesho & Ajio Integration)
- **Front Page Announcement Bar & Banner**: Clearly communicates to all visitors that products can be purchased directly on the website or on **Flipkart**, **Meesho**, and **Ajio**.
- **Marketplace Badges on Every Product**: Every product card and product detail view includes direct **Buy on Flipkart**, **Buy on Meesho**, and **Buy on Ajio** buttons with outbound referral tracking.
- **Admin Upload Tool**: When uploading or editing any product, you can paste the specific Flipkart, Meesho, and Ajio product links.

### 3. Complete E-Commerce Store Architecture
- **Website Basics**: Regal branding, responsive design, fast loading, search bar, floating WhatsApp concierge.
- **Main Collections**: Short Kurti, Long Kurti, 3pc Suit, Festive New Arrivals.
- **Product Page**: Multiple angle photos, 3D toggle, pricing, size selector, interactive Size Chart (inches & cm), fabric details, pincode delivery checker, 1-click WhatsApp order.
- **Cart & Checkout**: Slide-out cart drawer, quantity adjustment, coupon system (`AURA10`, `FESTIVE20`), free shipping progress bar, and complete checkout modal with **Instant UPI** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm QR), **Credit/Debit Card**, **Net Banking**, and **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
- **Order Celebration**: Confetti animation, Order Reference ID generation, and WhatsApp order tracking.
- **Policies**: 7-Day Return & Exchange, Shipping, Refund, Cancellation, Privacy, Terms, and Contact Us.
- **Admin Dashboard**:
  - Upload products with photo file uploads from computer or URLs
  - Edit / Delete products
  - Inventory & Stock adjustments
  - Order management & status updater
  - Marketplace referral analytics (Flipkart clicks vs Meesho clicks vs Ajio clicks vs Store Revenue)
  - 14-Point Pre-Launch Audit Checklist
